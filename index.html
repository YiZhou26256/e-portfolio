About Me

A little background on who I am and where I'm headed.

I am a postgraduate student majoring in Digital Education at Xi’an Jiaotong-Liverpool University. I completed my undergraduate studies in English Language and Literature, where I developed solid linguistic competence, systematic knowledge of English education, and a strong awareness of language teaching theories. During my undergraduate study, I obtained CET-4, CET-6, TEM-4 (Excellent) and TEM-8 (Good), which have equipped me with proficient bilingual capabilities for academic research and educational practice.

With a solid foundation in language education and ongoing training in digital pedagogy, educational psychology, and technology-enhanced learning, I am gradually building a learner-centred teaching perspective. My academic interests focus on inclusive education, learner developmental differences, and technology-assisted language instruction.

In the future, I aim to become an English educator working with adolescent and adult learners in international educational contexts and higher vocational education settings. Through this portfolio, I demonstrate my understanding of educational theories, reflective practice, and my ability to design inclusive, age-appropriate, and technology-supported teaching solutions for diverse learners.
